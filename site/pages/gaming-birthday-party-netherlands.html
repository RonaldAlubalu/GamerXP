Frequently asked questions

Is €275 not expensive for a kids' birthday party?

Fair question. A trip to the cinema for 8 children already costs €150 to €250 — but then you still have to drive, supervise the group and it is over after 1.5 hours. With GamerXperience you pay €275 for 3 hours of non-stop gaming at home, full setup and cleanup by us. That is €34 per child for the whole afternoon — less than most alternatives, and you do not have to go anywhere.

Do you need a lot of space?

No. A regular dining table for 6 to 8 people is enough. We adapt the setup to your space, whether you live in a city apartment or a spacious family home. We will let you know if we need anything specific when we confirm your booking.

I do not know anything about gaming — is that a problem?

Not at all. We connect everything, explain the games to the children and make sure everything works. You only need to provide snacks and drinks. With the Ultimate package our Game Master handles everything — you do not have to do a thing.

Do you travel to our city?

We travel all across the Netherlands. Based in Eindhoven, we regularly go to Utrecht, Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Den Haag, Den Bosch, Helmond and everywhere in between. Travel costs are never more than €100 and will be confirmed upfront when you book.

What is the difference between Standard and Ultimate?

Both packages include 8 gaming stations and full setup and cleanup. The main differences are: Ultimate gives you 4 hours instead of 3, includes a Game Master who runs a real tournament with challenges and a prize ceremony, and comes with thematic decoration like neon lights and banners. Standard is great for smaller groups who just want to game — Ultimate is the full experience.

Which games can the children play?

We bring a selection of the most popular games including Fortnite, Minecraft, Roblox, Rocket League, Fall Guys and more. All games are available through Xbox Game Pass. Have a specific game in mind? Let us know when you book and we will check if it is available.

Who comes to our door?

Ronald — founder of GamerXperience, father and former gaming journalist. We are a small, personal business. Not strangers, but someone who enjoys the party just as much as the children. With the Ultimate package Ronald is present the entire afternoon as Game Master.

How does payment work?

To confirm your date we ask for a €100 deposit. The remaining balance can be paid in advance by bank transfer, or in cash on the day of the party — whatever works best for you. With the Ultimate package there is no deposit for the equipment. Full details are in our Terms and Conditions.

Do you speak English?

Yes, we do. Feel free to contact us in English via WhatsApp or email. We are happy to help.
