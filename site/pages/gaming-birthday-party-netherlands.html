Frequently asked questions

Do you need a lot of space?

No. A large dining table (for 6 people) is enough. We set the screens up neatly side by side.

What do I need to arrange myself?

Three things: a large table, a power socket nearby and a working internet connection. We connect our own network to your router, preferably with a cable; good wifi works too. We bring the consoles, screens, controllers and games.

Who comes to our home?

Ronald Kers, the founder of Gamer Xperience. He is a father, a gamer and a former games journalist, and has hosted more than 50 parties. Ronald sets everything up himself, explains the games and packs up afterwards. With the Ultimate package he is there for the whole party as Game Master. Sometimes his son or his wife comes along to help.

I know nothing about gaming. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We connect everything, explain the games to the children and make sure it all works. You only take care of snacks and drinks.

Do you travel to our city?

We travel all across the Netherlands. From Eindhoven we regularly go to Utrecht, Amsterdam, Rotterdam, The Hague, Den Bosch and Helmond. Travel costs are never more than €100.

What is the difference between Standard and Ultimate?

Both include 8 gaming stations and full setup and cleanup. Ultimate gives you 4 hours instead of 3, a Game Master who runs a tournament with challenges and a prize ceremony, and themed decoration. Standard suits a group that just wants to game; Ultimate is the full experience.

Which games can the children play?

The most popular ones, including Fortnite, Minecraft, Roblox, Rocket League and Fall Guys. Have a specific game in mind? Tell us when you book and we will check whether it is available.

Is it not expensive for a birthday party?

Fair question. A cinema trip for 8 children soon costs €130 to €190 with popcorn and drinks, plus parking, and you drive, supervise the group and are done after an hour and a half. With Gamer Xperience you pay from €275 for 3 hours of gaming at home, with setup and cleanup by us. That is about €34 per child for the whole afternoon, and you do not have to go anywhere.

How does payment work?

A €100 deposit confirms your date. You pay the rest in advance by bank transfer, easily with the QR code on the invoice, or in cash on the day. With Ultimate there is no security deposit. The details are in our terms and conditions (in Dutch).

Do you speak English?

Yes. Contact us in English via WhatsApp or email. The booking form is in Dutch; if that is a hurdle, send us a message and we book it for you.
